CropCall AI

A hands-free, multilingual crop-incident workflow for Meta AI glasses.

AIFS × Meta Wearables AI AgTech Hackathon · May 2026 · Second place

Problem

We began with one question: where could Meta AI glasses create real value in agriculture?

A field worker may notice a crop issue while their hands are occupied. Identifying it is only the first step; the observation still has to reach someone who can act, sometimes across a language barrier.

What we built

  1. Capture a crop image with Meta AI glasses.
  2. Analyze the likely issue with Gemini.
  3. Turn the response into a structured incident report.
  4. Call a manager or specialist through Twilio.
  5. Support English-Spanish communication and return a live transcript.

The product evolved from crop diagnosis alone into a workflow for capture, communication, and human escalation.

My contribution

I contributed to use-case research and prototype iteration, implemented crop-identification and translation features using AI model APIs, and helped compare versions and select features for the final team demo.

Result

The team completed a working hackathon prototype and placed second. This was a short-build demonstration, not a field-validated production system; we do not claim measured accuracy, crop-loss reduction, or time savings.
