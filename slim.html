SLiM Replication Study

Testing whether one-shot LLM compression results remain consistent across evaluation and calibration settings.

UC Davis · Three-person research project · Jan–Mar 2026

Research question

Can SLiM's published compression behavior on LLaMA-2-13B be reproduced, and does it remain consistent when the calibration dataset changes?

Setup

Model
LLaMA-2-13B
Configuration
4-bit quantization · 50% sparsity
Compared
Dense · Wanda · SLiM
Calibration
C4 · Pile · CodeParrot
Completed tasks
ARC-Easy · ARC-Challenge · WinoGrande · OpenBookQA

Results and limitations

  • Under C4 and the four completed tasks, the aggregate ordering was Dense > SLiM > Wanda.
  • The Pile and CodeParrot comparison remains provisional: reused save paths and run settings mean those outputs are not fully independent evidence.
  • Two of six benchmark evaluations did not complete, so this is a partial replication rather than a full confirmation.

My contribution

I modified Python code and experiment settings from the published study and reran comparisons in Google Colab after Hugging Face setup issues. I studied the math and implementation to explain why the original settings performed better in the tested runs.
